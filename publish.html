SpecFormer: Spectral-aware Transformer for Cross-Domain Few-Shot Object Detection

Ziteng Wang,Qijun Chen,Xin Feng,Yunlong Wang,Zhenwei He, PRCV, 2026

Cross-domain few-shot object detection (CD-FSOD) aims to adapt detectors to novel classes with extremely limited annotations under severe domain shifts. Existing methods primarily operate in the spatial domain, where variations in texture, illumination, and background context introduce strong domain-specific interference, leading to unstable feature representations and degraded generalization. Recent studies explore frequency-domain representations for improved robustness; however, most rely on manually designed assumptions about domain-invariant frequency components, which can be unreliable in complex scenarios. To address this limitation, we propose SpecFormer, a spectral-aware Transformer framework for CD-FSOD that adaptively learns reliable frequency components. SpecFormer introduces Spectrally-Gated Hash Sparse Attention (SGHSA), which emphasizes informative spectral regions while suppressing domain-related noise, and Masked Background Spectral Mix (MB-SMix), which enhances background diversity and stabilizes spatial–spectral statistics under few-shot settings. Extensive experiments on multiple CD-FSOD benchmarks demonstrate that SpecFormer consistently outperforms state-of-the-art methods, achieving up to +2.3 mAP improvement over the previous best method under the 10-shot setting.

PSD-DualRA: PSD-Guided Dual-Domain RAW Adaptation for Low-Light Object Detection

Qijun Chen,Ziteng Wang,Xin Feng,Yunlong Wang,Di Ming, PRCV, 2026

Low-light images suffer from low brightness, reduced contrast, loss of details, and severe noise, which is often further amplified by ISP processing, especially degrading object boundaries and textures for small or low-texture targets. Existing spatial-only denoising methods struggle to model non-white noise with complex spectral characteristics. We propose PSD-Guided Dual-Domain RAW Adaptation (PSD-DualRA), a noise-aware RAW-domain framework for low-light object detection that jointly integrates denoising and ISP adaptation. The key idea is to explicitly model noise in the frequency domain using power spectral density (PSD), enabling structured characterization of noise across frequencies. Based on this, we learn the spectral shape of noise and suppress it via Wiener filtering. To further stabilize enhancement, we introduce a high-frequency spectral constraint to prevent excessive noise amplification. We also enforce spatial–frequency consistency and use denoised features to guide ISP parameter prediction, improving object structures while reducing background interference. Experiments on multiple RAW benchmarks demonstrate consistent detection improvements over strong baselines.

双向并行局部注意力视觉 Transformer 方法

Ziteng Wang,Junjie Wang,Xin Feng, 发明专利, CN118736295A, 2024

本发明公开了双向并行局部注意力视觉Transformer方法，涉及计算机视觉技术领域。本发明首先在特征层面对补丁进行分组，在每个组内执行局部注意力操作，有效利用特征空间中补丁之间的关系，弥补信息丢失的问题，其次，为了有效融合补丁之间的信息，将基于语义的局部注意力和基于图像的局部注意力进行并行结合，通过双向自适应学习来增强ViT模型在小数据集上的性能，实验结果表明，该方法在计算量为15.2GFLOPs和参数量为57.2M的情况下，分别在CIFAR‑10数据集以及CIFAR‑100数据集上实现了97.93％和85.80％的准确性，相较其他方法，双向并行局部注意力的视觉Transformer在增强局部引导能力的同时，保持了局部注意力所需属性的有效性。

基于角度信息对无人机有效定位的研究

王子腾,李西然，张佳怡，刘凯, 河南工程学院学报, 2024

无人机飞行技术对维护国家权益和捍卫国防安全具有重要意义，而纯方位无源定位是无人机编队调整和飞行的核心问题。针对发射信号无人机位置无偏差且编号已知的情况，给出了基于角度信息的无人机定位模型。在发射信号无人机位置无偏差情况下，除了指定两架编号特定的无人机，还需要一架无人机发射信号就可以实现接收信号无人机的有效定位。对于发射信号和接收信号无人机位置均略有偏差的问题，针对不同角度信息提出了具体的调整方案，使得无人机能够更加精确地到达指定位置，并对每个问题给出了相应的算例。
